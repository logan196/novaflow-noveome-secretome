Module 2 is an innate-immune / mucosal defense program

Top proteins by correlation with the module-2 eigengene. The module is built from the exact biology NEC trials measure: calprotectin (S100A8/S100A9), lactoferrin (LTF), lysozyme (LYZ).

Calprotectin (S100A8/A9) and fecal lactoferrin are studied clinical biomarkers in necrotizing enterocolitis (J Pediatr Surg 2018; NEC biomarkers systematic review, Front Pediatr 2025). This module is gestationally locked (ρ = +0.88, p ≈ 1×10-8): the developmental stage of the source material shifts the innate-immune payload.
